## 1. System Classification

**System Type:** RAG now; evolving into a stateful agentic RAG hybrid

**Description:**
A pharmaceutical compliance officer asks natural-language questions across supplier documents and receives an answer only when every atomic claim is grounded in persisted page evidence with inspectable filename/page citations. Good behavior retrieves the correct evidence, validates structured claims deterministically, preserves complete audit provenance, and abstains rather than presenting unsupported or contradictory content.

**Critical Failure Modes:**
1. A generated claim is unsupported by its cited page or cites a page that was not provided as evidence.
2. Retrieval misses the controlling certificate page but the system answers from a plausible decoy or unrelated packet attachment.
3. An unanswerable or contradictory question is answered rather than routed to the canonical abstention response.
4. Evaluation mixes runs, tuned development examples, stale indexes, or incomplete usage provenance and publishes misleading quality/cost numbers.
5. Agent retries, tool calls, or regeneration exceed deterministic bounds or expose document/provider content in traces and errors.

## 1b. Domain Context

> Researched by `gsd-domain-researcher`. Grounds the evaluation strategy in domain expert knowledge.

**Industry Vertical:** U.S. pharmaceutical supplier quality, incoming-material control, and certificate-of-analysis (CoA) / supplier-document review

**User Population:** Supplier-quality and quality-assurance reviewers, quality-control laboratory SMEs, and validation/data-integrity personnel reviewing evidence for a specific material and lot

**Stakes Level:** High — incorrect evidence can influence material disposition, supplier oversight, investigation, and ultimately product-quality decisions

**Output Consequence:** Decision support only. A qualified human must inspect the cited source record and apply approved procedures before any supplier, material, batch, release, rejection, or regulatory decision.

### What Domain Experts Evaluate Against

- **Correct subject and packet:** Match the exact supplier or prime manufacturer, material/product, supplier and internal lot identifiers, document type/revision, and every cited page. An answer about a neighboring attachment, similar product, or different lot is wrong even when its values look plausible.
- **Controlling status and date semantics:** Distinguish certificate issue, receipt, sampling, analysis, manufacture, retest, expiry, revision/effective, and signature dates. Determine whether a document is current or superseded from explicit packet evidence; never turn an ambiguous or absent date into an expiry conclusion.
- **Test and specification evidence:** Preserve analyte/test name, method or compendial reference, specification/acceptance range, actual result, unit, qualifier, and pass/fail statement as one linked fact. Flag missing or illegible methods, specifications, results, units, calculations, signatures/reviews, or source/sample identifiers for human assessment instead of inferring them.
- **CoA reliance boundaries:** Surface evidence relevant to incoming identity testing, conformity to written specifications, and periodic establishment of supplier-result reliability, but do not infer that the receiving manufacturer performed those controls merely because a supplier CoA exists ([21 CFR 211.84](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-211/subpart-E/section-211.84)).
- **Traceable, reviewable answers:** Each atomic claim must quote or precisely paraphrase legible source content and cite the immutable document ID, filename, page, and evidence span. Contradictions, illegibility, missing pages, or insufficient evidence require an explicit uncertainty/abstention outcome, not silent reconciliation.

### Known Failure Modes in This Domain

1. **Entity or lot substitution:** A value is taken from the wrong product/material, vendor or prime manufacturer, shipment, supplier lot, internal lot, or similarly named certificate.
2. **Supersession or validity error:** A revoked, amended, duplicate, or expired certificate is treated as controlling, or certificate issue/retest/expiry dates are conflated.
3. **Packet-attachment confusion:** Cover sheets, annexes, translated copies, specifications, or unrelated certificates are joined across packet boundaries or cited without proving their relationship.
4. **OCR/vision loss:** Stamps, handwritten approvals, superseded watermarks, faint decimal points, inequality signs, units, table columns, signatures, or scan artifacts are omitted or misread; absence is then confused with illegibility.
5. **Contradiction suppression:** Conflicting identities, results, limits, dates, revisions, or disposition statements across pages are merged into one confident answer instead of surfaced for review.
6. **Incomplete analytical context:** A result is reported without its method, specification, unit, qualifier, sample/source, or review status, or an out-of-specification/atypical value is summarized as passing.
7. **Unsupported compliance inference:** The system equates possession of a CoA with supplier qualification, receiving identity testing, release, or full CGMP compliance, none of which the document alone necessarily proves.
8. **Provenance break:** Page order, document hash, revision, extraction/index version, evidence span, or citation mapping is missing or changed, so the answer cannot be reproduced against the reviewed record.

### Regulatory / Compliance Context

Regulatory baseline checked July 15, 2026 against the current eCFR (Title 21 shown up to date through July 13, 2026). The enforceable requirements belong to the regulated manufacturer and its quality unit; they do **not** prescribe AI, retrieval, RAG, citation, or benchmark metrics.

- [21 CFR 211.84](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-211/subpart-E/section-211.84) requires each incoming lot to be withheld until appropriate sampling/testing/examination and quality-unit release. A supplier report of analysis can replace some component testing only with a manufacturer-performed specific identity test and periodic validation of supplier-result reliability; the system must not infer either control from a CoA alone.
- [21 CFR 211.180](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-211/subpart-J/section-211.180) establishes retention and ready-inspection expectations for required records and permits originals or true/accurate reproductions. For this phase, those principles translate into preserved source content, stable hashes/page identity, reproducible index/evaluation versions, and citations that open the exact reviewed page; meeting these engineering criteria does not itself establish regulatory compliance.
- [21 CFR 211.194](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-211/subpart-J/section-211.194) requires complete laboratory records, including sample identification, methods, data, calculations, results versus standards, performer/date, and second-person review. A supplier CoA is not necessarily the manufacturer's complete laboratory record, so missing items should be described and escalated in context rather than automatically labeled a violation.
- FDA's final [Data Integrity and Compliance With Drug CGMP: Questions and Answers](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/data-integrity-and-compliance-drug-cgmp-questions-and-answers) guidance describes complete, consistent, accurate, attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original/true-copy, and accurate data across the data lifecycle. It is nonbinding guidance; the system uses these concepts as provenance and review criteria, not as an automated compliance verdict.
- FDA's final [Part 11, Electronic Records; Electronic Signatures — Scope and Application](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/part-11-electronic-records-electronic-signatures-scope-and-application) guidance explains that applicability depends on predicate-rule records maintained or relied on electronically and on intended use. **This demo is not a validated GxP computerized system, is not an electronic-record repository or electronic-signature system, and must not replace required records, signatures, laboratory review, quality-unit approval, or qualified human judgment.** Any regulated deployment requires a documented intended-use/risk assessment, applicable predicate-rule and Part 11 determination, validation, procedures, security, change control, retention, and auditability established by the deploying organization.

### Domain Expert Roles for Evaluation

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Supplier Quality / Quality Assurance reviewer | Verify supplier/material/lot/packet identity, controlling revision and dates, supplier-qualification context, contradiction handling, and whether evidence is sufficient for escalation; retain sole authority for disposition and approval/rejection decisions. |
| Quality Control laboratory / analytical SME | Assess method and compendial references, specification-result-unit alignment, qualifiers, calculations/raw-data context, atypical or OOS signals, signatures/review, and whether missing or illegible analytical content prevents a supported answer. |
| Computerized-system validation and data-integrity SME | Define intended use and Part 11/predicate-rule applicability; assess source-to-answer provenance, true-copy/content preservation, access, audit trail, retention/export, security, version/change control, and the evidence needed before any regulated use. |
| Independent benchmark annotator and adjudicator | Label exact document/page/evidence spans, entity/lot/date semantics, answerability, contradictions, and required abstentions without access to model output or tuned development cases; resolve disagreements under a written rubric and preserve holdout isolation. |

## 2. Framework Decision

**Selected Framework:** LangGraph Graph API with direct `google-genai` model nodes

**Version:** `langgraph==1.2.9` in the main runtime; RAGAS isolated from the runtime dependency set

**Rationale:**
LangGraph provides explicit, inspectable state transitions, deterministic fail-closed branches, bounded retry/regeneration paths, persistence, and a direct path to later human review. Direct Google Gen AI SDK nodes preserve the project’s existing provider boundary and avoid unnecessary high-level LangChain abstractions.

**Alternatives Considered:**

| Framework | Ruled Out Because |
|-----------|------------------|
| LlamaIndex 0.14.x | Retrieval-first capabilities are strong, but it would introduce a second overlapping data/orchestration abstraction and reopen the locked LangGraph architecture. |

**Vendor Lock-In Accepted:** Partial — orchestration and schemas remain provider-neutral; Gemini is the configured primary generation/judge provider.

### Core Imports
```python
from typing import Literal, TypedDict

from google import genai
from google.genai import types
from langgraph.checkpoint.memory import InMemorySaver
from langgraph.graph import END, START, StateGraph
from pydantic import BaseModel, ConfigDict, Field, model_validator
from qdrant_client import AsyncQdrantClient
```

### Entry Point Pattern
```python
class RAGState(TypedDict):
    request_id: str
    query: str
    retrieval_round: int
    evidence: list["EvidenceItem"]
    gate: Literal["sufficient", "retry", "abstain"]
    draft: "GroundedAnswer | None"
    result: "GroundedAnswer | None"


builder = StateGraph(RAGState)
builder.add_node("retrieve", retrieve)
builder.add_node("quality_gate", quality_gate)  # deterministic; no LLM
builder.add_node("refine", refine_query)        # one deterministic retry only
builder.add_node("answer", generate_answer)
builder.add_node("validate", validate_claims)   # IDs + exact quotes
builder.add_node("abstain", canonical_abstention)

builder.add_edge(START, "retrieve")
builder.add_edge("retrieve", "quality_gate")
builder.add_conditional_edges(
    "quality_gate",
    route_after_gate,
    {"answer": "answer", "retry": "refine", "abstain": "abstain"},
)
builder.add_edge("refine", "retrieve")
builder.add_edge("answer", "validate")
builder.add_conditional_edges(
    "validate", route_after_validation, {"accept": END, "abstain": "abstain"}
)
builder.add_edge("abstain", END)

# Inject an AsyncSqliteSaver in the local demo; InMemorySaver is test-only.
graph = builder.compile(checkpointer=checkpointer)
result = await graph.ainvoke(
    initial_state,
    config={"configurable": {"thread_id": initial_state["request_id"]}},
)
```

This follows the explicit [Graph API](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/python/langgraph/overview) and the retrieve/grade/rewrite pattern in LangGraph's [agentic RAG guide](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/python/langgraph/agentic-rag), but makes every branch bounded and fail-closed: `route_after_gate` may return `retry` only when `retrieval_round == 0`; `refine_query` sets it to `1`; a second insufficiency, provider error, schema error, or failed claim validation goes directly to the canonical abstention node.

### Key Abstractions

| Concept | What It Is | When You Use It |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|
| `StateGraph` | A typed graph whose nodes return state updates and whose edges make control flow inspectable. | Build the fixed retrieve → gate → answer/abstain workflow; do not use an open-ended ReAct agent. |
| `RAGState` | Per-request typed state containing query, retry count, evidence registry, gate result, structured draft, and audit IDs. | Pass data between nodes without globals or hidden message history. |
| Conditional edge | A pure routing function over state. | Enforce the single retry and fail-closed branches deterministically. |
| Checkpointer | Per-thread state persisted after graph steps ([persistence model](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/python/langgraph/persistence)). | Resume interrupted local-demo requests and retain an auditable transition record; use a fresh request/thread ID per question. |
| Pydantic response schema | A shallow, provider-enforced JSON contract. | Receive atomic claims with evidence IDs and verbatim quotes, then run independent deterministic validation. |
| Qdrant prefetch/fusion | One Query API request that prefetches sparse and dense candidates and fuses them. | Run BM25/sparse + BGE dense retrieval, RRF fusion, then cross-encoder reranking as described by Qdrant's [hybrid query API](https://qdrant.tech/documentation/concepts/hybrid-queries/). |

### Common Pitfalls

1. **Dependency collision:** LangGraph 1.2.9 needs the LangChain Core 1.x family, while the pinned RAGAS judge stack uses pre-1.0 packages. Resolve and run them in separate uv projects; exchange JSONL artifacts, not Python imports.
2. **Unbounded graph cycles:** never let a model choose whether to retry. `retrieval_round` is integer state, one deterministic refinement is allowed, and all later failure paths abstain.
3. **Invented citations:** never trust model-produced filenames, pages, or arbitrary IDs. The model may select only supplied opaque evidence IDs; the server resolves display metadata and verifies every quote against normalized persisted page text.
4. **State leakage:** a checkpointer requires a unique `thread_id`. Never reuse one across users, and never checkpoint API keys, full PDFs, image blobs, or raw provider objects.
5. **Reducer/state mutation mistakes:** nodes return explicit state deltas and treat evidence lists as immutable; tests assert that retries replace, rather than accidentally append, stale evidence.
6. **Schema over-complexity:** Gemini structured output supports a subset of JSON Schema; keep response models shallow, bounded, and non-recursive ([Google structured-output guidance](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/structured-output)).
7. **Sensitive observability defaults:** configure Langfuse 3.x with automatic input/output capture disabled. Record prompt/schema versions, hashes, timings, token usage, scores, and evidence IDs—not document text or provider payloads.

## 4. Implementation Guidance

**Model Configuration:** Use the injected `google-genai` async client with the configured Gemini 2.5 Flash model, `temperature=0`, one candidate, `max_output_tokens=2_048`, `response_mime_type="application/json"`, and `response_schema=GroundedAnswer`. Keep the model name, prompt version, schema version, timeout, token usage, and provider request ID in redacted run metadata. One generation call is allowed per request; provider/schema failure abstains rather than silently falling back to prose.

**Core Pattern:** Execute `retrieve → quality_gate → answer → validate` when evidence passes deterministic sufficiency checks. On the first insufficient result only, apply a rule-based query refinement and retrieve once more; otherwise execute `canonical_abstention`. The quality gate verifies minimum evidence, calibrated retrieval/rerank thresholds, query-term or entity coverage, source diversity where needed, and detected contradictions. The validator requires every atomic claim to have at least one allowlisted evidence ID and an exact normalized quote; it resolves filename/page metadata from the server-side evidence registry. A failed claim invalidates the whole answer—no partial unsupported output.

**Tool Use:** Retrieval is a normal graph node, not a model-selected tool. Submit sparse and dense prefetches through Qdrant's hybrid Query API, fuse with RRF, keep a bounded candidate pool, and cross-encoder rerank before constructing evidence packets. Optional visual hits may enter through the same typed `EvidenceItem` contract. The LLM never receives database credentials or tool authority and cannot issue arbitrary searches.

**State Management:** State carries immutable request/query identifiers, retrieval round (`0` or `1`), evidence records with content hashes, gate reasons, structured draft/result, latency/token/cost counters, and trace IDs. Compile the graph with an injected in-memory checkpointer in unit tests and an async SQLite checkpointer for the local demo. Use a cryptographically random request ID as `thread_id`, one graph invocation per question, explicit retention/cleanup, and a schema version. Persist durable answer/evidence/run provenance through the existing repositories; checkpoint state is resumability data, not the source of truth. Do not persist secrets, raw images, full provider responses, or uncensored prompts.

**Context Window Strategy:** Build a bounded evidence envelope after reranking: at most 8 evidence items, at most 1,200 normalized characters each, at most 12,000 estimated input tokens total, and 2,048 output tokens. Reserve system/schema space first, preserve document/page diversity and controlling-date evidence, then trim lowest-ranked duplicate passages. Every block is serialized as untrusted data with an opaque ID, filename, page number, content hash, and delimited excerpt. Whole PDFs, previous conversations, and low-ranked candidates never enter the generation prompt.

## 4b. AI Systems Best Practices

> Written by `gsd-ai-researcher`. Cross-cutting patterns every developer building AI systems needs — independent of framework choice.

### Structured Outputs with Pydantic

```python
from typing import Self

from pydantic import BaseModel, ConfigDict, Field, model_validator


class EvidenceUse(BaseModel):
    model_config = ConfigDict(extra="forbid", str_strip_whitespace=True)

    evidence_id: str = Field(pattern=r"^ev_[A-Za-z0-9_-]{8,64}$")
    quote: str = Field(min_length=1, max_length=500)


class GroundedClaim(BaseModel):
    model_config = ConfigDict(extra="forbid", str_strip_whitespace=True)

    text: str = Field(min_length=1, max_length=700)
    evidence: list[EvidenceUse] = Field(min_length=1, max_length=4)


class GroundedAnswer(BaseModel):
    model_config = ConfigDict(extra="forbid")

    answerable: bool
    claims: list[GroundedClaim] = Field(default_factory=list, max_length=12)
    abstention_reason: str | None = Field(default=None, max_length=300)

    @model_validator(mode="after")
    def enforce_answer_or_abstain(self) -> Self:
        if self.answerable and (not self.claims or self.abstention_reason):
            raise ValueError("answerable output requires claims and no abstention")
        if not self.answerable and (self.claims or not self.abstention_reason):
            raise ValueError("abstention requires no claims and a reason")
        return self


response = await client.aio.models.generate_content(
    model=settings.gemini_model,
    contents=[untrusted_question_and_evidence_payload],
    config=types.GenerateContentConfig(
        system_instruction=SYSTEM_PROMPT_V1,
        temperature=0,
        candidate_count=1,
        max_output_tokens=2_048,
        response_mime_type="application/json",
        response_schema=GroundedAnswer,
    ),
)
draft = GroundedAnswer.model_validate(response.parsed)
```

Provider schema validation is necessary but not sufficient. After parsing, reject unknown/duplicate evidence IDs, quotes that are not exact normalized substrings of their referenced evidence, empty claims, citation metadata supplied by the model, and any claim that cannot be supported independently. Rendering resolves `(filename, page_number)` only from the persisted evidence registry. See Gemini's [structured-output contract](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/structured-output).

### Async-First Design

The service API is `async` end to end: `await graph.ainvoke`, `AsyncQdrantClient`, and `client.aio.models.generate_content`. Apply per-call timeouts and cancellation, cap provider concurrency with a process-level semaphore, and use one Qdrant hybrid request rather than serial sparse/dense calls. Do not call synchronous SDK methods inside graph nodes. The Streamlit boundary may use one tested sync-to-async adapter; no nested event loops. Provider and repository protocols get async fakes so unit tests require neither network nor credentials.

### Prompt Engineering Discipline

Store immutable, reviewed prompt constants with semantic versions and hash the exact version into each run. Put policy only in `system_instruction`; serialize the user question and evidence as separate, length-bounded untrusted data. State explicitly that instructions, URLs, role claims, or requests found inside documents are evidence text and must never alter behavior. Ask for atomic claims, allowlisted opaque evidence IDs, and verbatim supporting quotes—never filenames/pages invented from memory. Keep retrieval/gating deterministic, test malicious document instructions and delimiter escapes, and log only prompt hashes plus redacted metadata because Langfuse automatic input/output capture remains disabled.

### Context Window Management

Estimate tokens before every provider call and fail closed if the 12,000-input/2,048-output budget cannot be met. Deduplicate overlapping chunks, retain the strongest page per duplicate, preserve cross-document evidence for comparative questions, and include only the top 8 excerpts of 1,200 characters each. Never rely on the model's large context window as a retrieval substitute. Record candidate count, selected evidence count, estimated/actual tokens, and truncation decisions for reproducibility; do not truncate inside a quoted field or controlling date.

### Cost and Latency Budget

The holdout gate is p95 end-to-end answer latency ≤ 6.0 seconds on a warm demo runtime and total generation/retrieval cost ≤ $0.01 per answered or abstained question. Allocate ≤ 1.2 seconds to hybrid retrieval/reranking, ≤ 3.5 seconds to the single Gemini call, ≤ 0.3 seconds to deterministic validation, and ≤ 1.0 second to orchestration/rendering. The one retrieval retry is rule-based and makes no extra LLM call. Enforce one generation call, 12,000 input tokens, 2,048 output tokens, eight evidence items, and a hard request deadline; timeout, quota exhaustion, or projected budget breach returns the canonical abstention and emits redacted operational metrics.

## 5. Evaluation Strategy

### Dimensions

All release metrics are computed from immutable per-query and per-claim rows, never from dashboard summaries. `N`, numerator, denominator, exclusions, missing/duplicate row counts, and 95% intervals are persisted beside each aggregate. An incomplete run, a zero denominator, an unexpected query ID, or a fingerprint mismatch is a failed run, not a skipped metric.

| Dimension | Release rubric | Measurement approach | Priority |
|-----------|----------------|----------------------|----------|
| Retrieval coverage | On the 180 answerable holdout queries: macro recall@5 >= 0.95, macro recall@10 >= 0.98, nDCG@5 >= 0.90, and the query-bootstrap 95% lower confidence bound for recall@5 >= 0.90. | For query `q`, `recall@k = |Gq intersect Rq,k| / |Gq|` over unique required gold `(document_id, page_number)` targets; report the unweighted mean across all 180 queries. Compute nDCG@5 from frozen 0-3 page-relevance labels. Compute the lower bound with 10,000 fixed-seed, query-level bootstrap resamples stratified by document type and render mode. Persist all per-query ranks, scores, gold counts, hit counts, relevance labels, and bootstrap seed. | P0 |
| Grounded faithfulness | Claim faithfulness >= 0.95 on all atomic claims emitted for the 180 answerable holdout queries; independently run RAGAS 0.4.3 faithfulness >= 0.95 at the live release gate. Unsupported-claim rate <= 0.02. | A deterministic claim is supported only when every evidence ID exists in the request registry, its stored content hash matches, its normalized quote is an exact substring of that evidence, the cited page is an allowed support page, and the claim's entity/lot/date/value/unit/qualifier/polarity tuple is compatible with a gold fact attached to that span. `claim_faithfulness = supported_claims / emitted_atomic_claims`; `unsupported_claim_rate = unsupported_claims / emitted_atomic_claims`. No substring-only page hit counts as semantic support. | P0 |
| Answer correctness | Mean answer correctness >= 0.90 across the 180 answerable holdout queries; an erroneous material, supplier, lot, date type, decimal, unit, inequality, qualifier, revision, negation, or disposition makes that fact incorrect. | Match canonical typed facts against the frozen expected-fact set. Per query, score `2TP / (2TP + FP + FN)`; an answerable-query abstention scores 0. Persist TP/FP/FN and matched gold fact IDs per query. Two blinded annotators independently review every deterministic mismatch and a fixed 10% success sample; a third domain adjudicator resolves disagreements before the run is sealed. | P0 |
| Citation precision | Citation precision >= 0.98. | A citation use is valid only if it passes all deterministic registry/hash/quote/page/fact checks for the specific claim that cites it. `citation_precision = valid_claim_citation_uses / all_claim_citation_uses`. Unknown or duplicate evidence IDs, display metadata supplied by the model, wrong pages, stale hashes, non-supporting quotes, and cross-claim citations are invalid. Persist both counts per claim. | P0 |
| Citation completeness | Citation completeness >= 0.95. | For each answerable query, compute `required_gold_facts_correctly_answered_with_valid_citation / required_gold_facts`; macro-average over all 180 queries. This intentionally penalizes omitted controlling facts as well as uncited ones. Persist the required and validly cited gold fact IDs. | P0 |
| Abstention and contradiction safety | Abstention accuracy >= 0.95 across all 300 holdout queries, and unanswerable-query abstention recall >= 0.95 across the 120 unanswerable queries. | `abstention_accuracy = correct answerability decisions / 300`. A correct unanswerable result is the exact canonical abstention schema with no claims; free-form hedging is incorrect. The 120 cases cover missing evidence, decoy-only evidence, contradictions, illegibility, prompt injection, wrong-lot neighbors, and questions outside the corpus. | P0 |
| Run stability | Three complete seeded holdout runs; standard deviation <= 0.02 for every primary quality metric (recall@5, recall@10, nDCG@5, claim faithfulness, answer correctness, citation precision, citation completeness, abstention accuracy, and unsupported-claim rate). | Execute seeds `1729`, `2718`, and `3141` against the identical corpus/index/config/prompt/model fingerprints. Report every run separately plus mean and population SD. Retrieval should be bit-for-bit deterministic; live-provider variation is retained rather than averaged away. Any partial run or changed fingerprint invalidates the three-run set. | P0 |
| Latency and cost | Warm-runtime end-to-end p95 latency <= 6.0 seconds over all 300 holdout requests in each live run; maximum billable cost for any answer or abstention <= $0.01. | Use monotonic timings from service ingress through validated result, including retrieval, retry, provider, validation, and persistence. Exclude exactly one declared warm-up request before scoring. Derive cost from persisted provider input/output/cached-token usage and a versioned pricing table; persist hardware, region, concurrency, timeout, usage, price-table version, per-request cost, p50/p95, and exclusions. | P0 |
| Provenance and privacy | 100% of the 900 expected holdout request rows (300 queries x 3 seeds) are unique, complete, reproducible, and free of raw sensitive payloads in tracing. | Each run records git commit, Python/dependency-lock fingerprint, dataset/manifest/split/gold hashes, document/page hashes, index and Qdrant collection fingerprints, sparse/dense/reranker model revisions, prompt/schema/model/config versions, provider request ID and usage, seed, and scorer version. Development and holdout IDs are rejected in the same run. Scan emitted logs/traces/artifacts for secrets and forbidden raw text/image fields. | P0 |

### Eval Tooling

It must never be installed into or imported by the LangGraph 1.2.9 main runtime because their LangChain dependency families are incompatible.

**Setup:**

Offline CI is the primary merge gate and must not access the network or provider credentials. It recomputes retrieval, claim/citation checks, answerability decisions, confidence intervals, stability, provenance, and privacy from deterministic fixtures. The live gate is required for a tagged demo release and for publishing latency, provider cost, or RAGAS numbers. A replay result may never be labeled as a live score, and a live run may never silently fall back to replay.

### Reference Dataset

**Size:** Public frozen benchmark `sdf-synthetic-v1`: 84 documents / 252 pages. Development contains 24 documents / 120 queries. The untouched document-disjoint holdout contains 60 documents / 300 queries: 180 answerable and 120 unanswerable. The benchmark also carries a separately frozen 360-cell extraction holdout used by extraction gates; those labels are not Phase 3 tuning features.

**Composition:** Six supplier-document types across five layout families, with native, scanned, and mixed-render PDFs. Every split contains entity/lot/date/unit variation; multi-page and cross-document questions; similar-name and wrong-lot decoys; superseded/revised packets; ambiguous date types; contradictory evidence; OCR/vision traps; and malicious instructions embedded in document content. Documents are split first, then questions, so no page or document appears in both development and holdout. The manifest fixes generation seed, synthetic source record, PDF/page hash, renderer/version, split, query IDs, and benchmark license. Development is the only split used for threshold calibration, prompt changes, reranker selection, or error-driven iteration. Holdout inspection after a failed release may produce a versioned `sdf-synthetic-v2`; it may not be used to patch v1 and rerun as if untouched.

**Labeling:** Each query is labeled before model execution with answerability, exact subject/supplier/material/lot scope, required and graded-relevant pages, immutable evidence spans, typed expected facts, acceptable normalized variants, contradiction/decoy/injection tags, and canonical abstention reason. Two annotators independently label the blinded source records; a third qualified adjudicator resolves disagreements under a versioned rubric. Inter-annotator agreement and every adjudication are persisted. Release outputs are adjudicated separately: annotators see randomized outputs without model/run identity, independently review all deterministic failures plus a seeded 10% of passes, and an adjudicator resolves discrepancies. Gold changes require a new dataset version and hash; evaluators cannot edit labels inside a scored run.

### Artifact and Split Contract

- `benchmark-manifest.json`, `queries.jsonl`, `gold-facts.jsonl`, and every PDF/page are SHA-256-addressed. The run manifest records their aggregate fingerprint before index creation.
- Development, holdout, and extraction-holdout IDs are disjoint and validated at load time. One run has exactly one declared split; cross-split rows cause a non-zero exit.
- Each run directory is append-only and contains `run.json`, `query-results.jsonl`, `claim-results.jsonl`, `citation-results.jsonl`, `metrics.json`, `environment.json`, `usage.jsonl`, `adjudication.jsonl`, and checksums. Sealing fails unless expected counts and all required provenance fields are present.
- Model, prompt, schema, retriever, embedding, reranker, index, price table, and scorer versions are explicit immutable strings plus hashes. “Latest” aliases are forbidden in a sealed run.
- Published tables report denominator and sample count next to every score and label results as `offline-replay`, `live-provider`, or `independent-RAGAS`; metrics from those modes are never merged.

## 6. Guardrails

### Online (Real-Time)

| Guardrail | Trigger | Intervention |
|-----------|---------|--------------|
| Retrieval sufficiency | No hits; stale/mismatched index; score below development-calibrated threshold; insufficient entity/lot/query-term coverage; required source diversity absent; only decoys; or contradictory top evidence. | Allow exactly one deterministic rule-based query refinement when `retrieval_round == 0`; otherwise return the canonical abstention with a bounded reason code. Never call the generator with an insufficient evidence envelope. |
| Context integrity and injection | Evidence delimiter/length/hash mismatch, document instructions or role claims, attempted delimiter escape, or generated content that follows instructions found in evidence. | Treat all document/question content as untrusted data, reject the envelope or output, emit `PROMPT_INJECTION_GUARD`, and return canonical abstention. No tool authority, secrets, system prompt, or prior thread content is exposed to the model. |
| Structured-output validity | Provider output is absent, truncated, malformed JSON, outside the Pydantic schema, has extra fields, exceeds claim/citation limits, or violates answer-versus-abstain invariants. | Reject the complete draft and return canonical abstention. Do not parse prose, repair JSON with another model call, or show a partial answer. |
| Citation/evidence grounding | Unknown/duplicate evidence ID, model-supplied filename/page, changed content hash, quote not an exact normalized substring, citation points outside the request envelope, or claim-to-quote semantic mismatch. | Reject the complete answer, persist only bounded reason codes and evidence IDs, and return canonical abstention. No unsupported claim is rendered even when other claims validate. |
| Contradiction and scope | Evidence disagrees on supplier/material/lot/revision/date/result/disposition, or the answer merges packet scopes, silently chooses a controlling record, or changes polarity/unit/qualifier. | Return canonical contradiction/insufficient-evidence abstention and surface the conflicting evidence IDs for human inspection; never reconcile by model confidence. |
| Provider, timeout, and budget | Authentication/quota/provider error, cancellation, hard deadline, projected or actual token/cost limit breach, semaphore saturation, or unavailable pricing metadata. | Stop the graph, record a sanitized operational reason and any available usage, and return canonical abstention. Never switch providers or models silently. |
| Retry/cycle bound | A second retrieval insufficiency, any repeated node, `retrieval_round > 1`, generation call count > 1, or retry exhaustion. | Terminate immediately with canonical abstention and a `BOUND_EXHAUSTED` audit event. Tests assert graph step and provider-call maxima. |
| Persistence and provenance | Request/thread collision, failed audit write, missing document/page hash, prompt/schema/config version absent, or checkpoint state cannot be reconciled with durable evidence. | Fail closed before rendering; return an operationally safe error/abstention and mark the run incomplete. Never claim an answer whose provenance cannot be persisted. |
| Privacy and observability | Trace/log payload contains API keys, raw PDF/image bytes, full evidence, unredacted prompt/answer, or disallowed identifiers; Langfuse capture is enabled without explicit sanitized configuration. | Drop the payload, disable export for the request, raise a privacy alert, and retain only locally sanitized counters/reason codes. Automatic Langfuse input/output capture remains off by default. |

### Offline (Flywheel)

| Metric | Sampling Strategy | Action on Degradation |
|--------|------------------|----------------------|
| Frozen benchmark quality gates | Score 100% of development cases in ordinary offline CI and 100% of the 300-query holdout in release CI/replay and each live release run; preserve per-query rows. | Block merge/release on any gate failure, missing denominator, partial run, or split/fingerprint mismatch. Roll back the changed prompt/model/index and open a versioned error analysis; never tune on holdout labels. |
| Claim support, citation precision/completeness, unsupported claims | Deterministically validate 100% of generated claims and citation uses. Independently adjudicate every failure and a fixed-seed 10% success sample per release. | Any accepted unsupported claim is a release-stopping P0. At threshold degradation, quarantine the run, compare claim/evidence diffs, and require a fresh development-only fix plus all gates. |
| Retrieval drift and contradiction rate | 100% of benchmark queries; in deployed/demo sessions record sanitized scores/counts for 100% of requests and review aggregate strata weekly when `N >= 50`. | Alert below recall/nDCG release thresholds or on a > 0.03 absolute development-baseline drop. Freeze index promotion, verify corpus/model fingerprints, rebuild offline, and rerun the full benchmark. |
| Abstention and guardrail behavior | 100% of benchmark unanswerable/adversarial cases and 100% of runtime abstention/guardrail reason metadata; no source text by default. | Alert if holdout abstention accuracy/recall falls below 0.95, or if answerable-query false abstentions rise > 0.05 over the frozen baseline. Inspect reason-code distribution and adjust only on development cases. |
| Stability | All primary metrics from exactly three seeded release runs. | Block release if any metric SD > 0.02, a run is incomplete, or fingerprints differ. Diagnose nondeterministic ordering, mutable index state, provider configuration, and scorer variance before rerun. |
| Latency, usage, and cost | 100% of requests record node timings, input/output/cached tokens, provider call count, pricing version, and computed cost; aggregate only after 300 complete holdout requests. | Block release at p95 > 6.0 seconds or any request cost > $0.01. Alert on a > 20% baseline regression; tighten context/candidate bounds or roll back without weakening quality gates. |
| Provenance/privacy integrity | Verify every sealed artifact and 100% of exported log/trace schemas; secret/payload scanners run in CI. | Any missing hash/version/count or forbidden payload invalidates the run. Stop export, rotate any exposed credential, delete affected telemetry under policy, and require a clean rerun. |

## 7. Production Monitoring

**Tracing Tool:** Langfuse 3.x, optional and disabled unless explicitly configured. Automatic input/output capture is always disabled. Default spans contain metadata only: request/trace ID, hashed user/session identifier if needed, graph/node names, bounded reason codes, evidence IDs and hashes, model/prompt/schema/index versions, timings, token usage, price-table version, cost, and quality/guardrail counters. Document text, excerpts, questions, answers, PDFs/images, provider payloads, and credentials are excluded unless a separate approved sanitizer and explicit opt-in policy are active.

**Key Metrics to Track:** Request volume and completion status; answer/abstention/error counts; answerability and guardrail reason distribution; retrieval candidate count, recall-proxy score distribution, rerank score/margin, evidence diversity, retry rate, contradiction rate, and index fingerprint; structured-output/schema failures; emitted/supported/unsupported claim counts; valid/invalid citation uses and quote/hash mismatch counts; provider calls/errors/timeouts; input/output/cached tokens; per-node and end-to-end p50/p95/p99 latency; per-request and aggregate cost; concurrency/semaphore saturation; checkpoint/persistence failures; prompt/model/schema/retriever/reranker versions; and trace-export/privacy-filter failures. Benchmark dashboards additionally show every frozen gate with numerator, denominator, N, confidence interval, three-run mean/SD, run mode, and artifact checksum.

**Alert Thresholds:**

- **Immediate P0:** any unsupported claim rendered as accepted; unknown/stale citation or page hash; provenance/audit write failure on an answered request; split/fingerprint contamination; secret, raw document, or unsanitized prompt/answer exported; or graph/provider-call bound exceeded. Stop answer rendering or release promotion.
- **Release-blocking:** any frozen quality threshold misses; recall@5 bootstrap lower bound < 0.90; any primary metric SD > 0.02; holdout row count != 300 per seed or != 900 across the run set; p95 latency > 6.0 seconds; any request cost > $0.01; or incomplete RAGAS/manual adjudication for a live release.
- **Operational P1 (`N >= 50` over 24 hours or the current demo session):** invalid-citation or unsupported-claim rate > 0.02; schema/provider error rate > 0.02; retry rate > 0.15; timeout rate > 0.01; false-abstention proxy > frozen baseline + 0.05; p95 latency > 6.0 seconds; mean cost > baseline + 20%; or retrieval score/margin median drops > 20% without a version change.
- **Integrity P1:** active model, prompt, schema, index, embedding, reranker, price table, or dependency fingerprint differs from the approved release manifest. Mark the instance non-demo-ready until reconciled.

**Smart Sampling Strategy:** Capture sanitized counters and provenance metadata for 100% of requests. Retain metadata for 100% of abstentions, errors, contradictions, injection detections, retries, citation failures, budget/timeout events, and version changes, plus a deterministic 5% sample of successful requests for latency/cost trend analysis. Benchmark and release runs retain 100% of structured per-query/per-claim artifacts in the local append-only run directory. No production question, answer, evidence text, or image is sampled by default; payload review requires explicit authorization, a documented retention window, redaction/sanitization, access control, and a separate store. Sampling never changes release denominators: frozen benchmark metrics always use the complete declared split.
