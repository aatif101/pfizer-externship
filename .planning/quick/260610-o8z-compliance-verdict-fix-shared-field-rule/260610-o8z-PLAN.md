<objective>
Implement two user-approved decisions (made 2026-06-10) to fix the Pfizer SDF compliance verdict on real scanned documents:

1. **Shared field rulebook** — One labeling guideline (`docs/field-definitions.md`) governing both extraction prompts AND gold labels. Allows documented synonyms (e.g. "Approved On" for effective_date), treats printed "N/A" expiry as the literal value, uses full legal vendor names, while keeping existing trap-date exclusions.
2. **Visual evidence tier (supersedes D026)** — When a cited page's stored text is EMPTY (scanned pages where verbatim grounding is mathematically impossible), accept the image-grounded value tagged `evidence_type='visual'` with `needs_review` forced on. Stored-text pages keep the existing verbatim-grounded `evidence_type='text'` contract unchanged. A failed match against NON-empty text still abstains.

Purpose: D026 made the M004 visual fallback structurally unable to help on scanned pages (page_text length 0). The visual tier preserves the no-hallucination story: text claims are verbatim-verified, visual claims are page-cited + flagged for review.
Output: Field rulebook doc, updated prompts, additive `evidence_type` schema/model/persistence path, idempotent gold relabel script, new DECISIONS.md row, before/after eval results.
</objective>
